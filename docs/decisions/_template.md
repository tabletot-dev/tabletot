# NNNN — Title (short, declarative; "We use a monorepo", not "Monorepo decision")

## Context

What's the situation that forced this decision? What constraints, prior
decisions, or external pressures shaped it? Anyone reading this in six
months should be able to understand *why* a decision was needed without
hunting through chat logs.

Keep it factual. No framing or persuasion yet.

## Decision

What did we decide? Stated plainly, in one or two sentences if possible.
Then any necessary clarifications (scope, what's included, what's not).

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table? For each, one or two lines on why we didn't
pick it. Not exhaustive — just the ones that were genuinely competitive.
The point is to capture that we *knew* about them, so future-us doesn't
"discover" an option we'd already rejected.

- **Alternative A** — why not.
- **Alternative B** — why not.

## Consequences

What does this commit us to? What gets easier, what gets harder, what
becomes load-bearing? What would force us to revisit this decision?

This is the most under-written section in most ADRs. Don't skip it —
it's the part future-you will thank present-you for.
